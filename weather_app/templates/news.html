About this page

El Niño news and weather news from around the world in many languages: storms, floods, heat, monsoon, drought and more, plus humanitarian reports, agency updates, new research, discussions, podcasts and videos, all from free public sources.

Where this comes from

Everything is collected automatically every 30 minutes from free sources that need no account. Links open the original publisher. Topics, countries and crops are tagged from the headline, so a few tags can be wrong.

Sources include Google News, Bing News, GDELT, ReliefWeb, NOAA Climate.gov, UN News, arXiv, Crossref, Hacker News, Apple Podcasts and YouTube channels of weather agencies. Back to the El Niño Tracker.
